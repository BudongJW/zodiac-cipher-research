## 3. 学术论文

### 3.1 直接研究 Zodiac 密文

- **Oranchak, D., Blake, S., Van Eycke, J.** “The Solution of the Zodiac Killer's 340-Character Cipher.” *arXiv:2403.17350* [cs.AI; cs.CR], 2024-03-26. DOI: [10.48550/arXiv.2403.17350](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2403.17350)
  —— Z340 破解者本人撰写的完整技术报告（同音替换 + 换位），附大量参考文献。
  - 网上流传的“Cryptologia 48(2):129–157”期刊版本 **【待核实】**：Crossref 中查无此记录，疑为误传。
- **von zur Gathen, J.** “Unicity distance of the Zodiac-340 cipher.” *Cryptologia* 47(5):474–488, 2023. DOI: [10.1080/01611194.2023.2174821](https://doi.org/10.1080/01611194.2023.2174821)
  —— 计算 Z340 的唯一解距离（HistoCrypt 2022 版给出区间 80–152），从信息论角度支持 2020 年解的唯一性。早期版本：IACR ePrint [2021/1620](https://eprint.iacr.org/2021/1620)；HistoCrypt 2022, DOI [10.3384/ecp188395](https://doi.org/10.3384/ecp188395)。
- **Raddum, H., Sýs, M.** “The Zodiac Killer Ciphers.” *Tatra Mountains Mathematical Publications* 45(1):75–91, 2010. DOI: [10.2478/v10127-010-0007-8](https://doi.org/10.2478/v10127-010-0007-8)
  —— 2020 年前即证明 Z340 符号序列强烈偏离随机，并提出攻击思路。
- **Juzek, T. S.** “Using the Entropy of N-Grams to Evaluate the Authenticity of Substitution Ciphers and Z340 in Particular.” *HistoCrypt 2019*, LECP 158:13, pp. 117–125. [PDF](https://ep.liu.se/ecp/158/013/ecp19158013.pdf)
  —— 以 n-gram 熵判定 Z340“不是单纯替换”，后被换位层的发现所印证。
- **King, J. C., Bahler, D. R.** “An Algorithmic Solution of Sequential Homophonic Ciphers.” *Cryptologia* 17(2):148–165, 1993. DOI: [10.1080/0161-119391867827](https://doi.org/10.1080/0161-119391867827)
  —— REMOVE_HOMOPHONES 算法，利用“同音字按顺序轮换”这一 Z408 的已知弱点。
- **King, J. C., Bahler, D. R.** “A Framework for the Study of Homophonic Ciphers in Classical Encryption and Genetic Systems.” *Cryptologia* 17(1):45–54, 1993. DOI: [10.1080/0161-119391867755](https://doi.org/10.1080/0161-119391867755)
  —— 提出“multiplicity（密文字母表大小 ÷ 密文长度）”概念，对 Z13/Z32 的可解性分析至关重要。
- **Oranchak, D.** “Evolutionary algorithm for decryption of monoalphabetic homophonic substitution ciphers encoded as constraint satisfaction problems.” *GECCO 2008*, pp. 1717–1718. DOI: [10.1145/1389095.1389425](https://doi.org/10.1145/1389095.1389425)
- **Mamro, O., Lagun, A., Dupak, B.** “Investigation of Homophonic Encryption on Zodiac Z408 and Z340 Ciphers.” *IEEE ELIT 2021*, pp. 109–112. DOI: [10.1109/ELIT53502.2021.9501114](https://doi.org/10.1109/ELIT53502.2021.9501114)
- **Shapiro, R. B.** “A Brief Guide to the Authentication of Cryptanalytic Claims.” *HistoCrypt 2026*, Tartu University Library. [馆藏页](https://dspace.ut.ee/collections/70b1d875-afc3-46a7-819a-cb425b29689d)
  —— 用信息论检验“破译声明”的可信度，特别关注短密文 —— 与 Z13/Z32 直接相关。（条目 handle **【待核实】**）
